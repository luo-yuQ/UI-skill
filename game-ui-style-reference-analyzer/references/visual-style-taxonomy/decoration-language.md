# Decoration Language

## Analyze

Record visible ornamental motifs and their density, symmetry, scale, and restrained/ornate tendency.

## Direct observation

Repeated runes, rivets, vines, crystals, carved forms, scrollwork, or other motifs and their spatial organization are observable.

## Describe

Name only visible motifs and summarize organization with comparable fields. Keep subject-specific emblems in `content_specific_traits` when they may be local to one character or object.

## Common errors

- Treating functional seams or edges as ornament without evidence.
- Assuming one emblem is a global motif.
- Recommending decorative elements for buttons, panels, or pages.

## Do not analyze

Do not prescribe ornament for future UI, infer confirmed faction lore, or propose reusable decoration assets.
