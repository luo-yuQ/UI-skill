# Material Language

## Analyze

Record visible or cautiously inferred materials and their surface qualities, such as rough/smooth, matte/glossy, worn/clean, hard/soft, or reflective/subdued.

## Direct observation

Highlights, texture, translucency, surface wear, and edge behavior are observable. A named material may be inferred when those cues support it.

## Describe

Pair a material name with short qualities, for example “silver metal: hard, reflective, clean.” Lower confidence or use uncertainty when the substance cannot be distinguished reliably.

## Common errors

- Treating color alone as proof of a material.
- Calling every reflective surface metal or every translucent surface glass.
- Turning material observations into implementation or asset-production advice.

## Do not analyze

Do not prescribe shaders, texture maps, engine settings, or materials for future UI elements.
